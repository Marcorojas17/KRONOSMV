# Human Vision — Document 001

**Author:** Marco Antonio Rojas Valdovinos
**Date:** 2026-09-15

## What I meant to say

To leave a public record of a phenomenon that almost no one is naming:
the next monopoly will not be of products or prices, but of the
infrastructure through which we think.

## Why it matters

Because it does not feel like a monopoly. It feels like default.
It feels like "this is just how things are." And when something feels
that way, it is no longer questioned.

## What I do not want lost

The seal. The hour. The signature. The idea that this was said by a human
with a name, on a date, from a place.

## What this document is NOT

Not an academic paper. Not a political manifesto. Not a prediction.
It is a record. A minute.
