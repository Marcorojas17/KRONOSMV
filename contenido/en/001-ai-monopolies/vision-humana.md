## What I meant to say

To leave a public record of a phenomenon that almost no one is naming:
the next monopoly will not be of products or prices, but of the
infrastructure through which we think.

## Why it matters

Because it does not feel like a monopoly. It feels like default.
And when something feels that way, it is no longer questioned.
