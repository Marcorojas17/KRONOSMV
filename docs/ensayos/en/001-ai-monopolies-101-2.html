AI Monopolies That Do Not Exist Yet

AuthorMarco Antonio Rojas Valdovinos Date2026-09-15 LanguageEnglish Versionv1.0.0

Abstract

An analysis of the emergence of a new type of digital monopoly: one that controls neither products nor prices, but the infrastructure through which we think.

1. The frontier AI monopoly

  • NVIDIA controls chips, software (CUDA), and interconnect networks (NVLink).
  • OpenAI warns that power may concentrate in a single person, company, or country.
  • Lock-in occurs not through price, but through switching cost.

2. The cloud as public utility

  • AWS and Azure operate as a consolidated oligopoly.
  • The EU seeks to regulate them under the Digital Markets Act (DMA).

3. Vertical integration

Silicon → Network → Cloud → Model → Agent → Distribution

4. Behavioral power

Power is no longer measured by price, but by conduct.

5. Containment principles

  1. Mandatory interoperability.
  2. Open AI.
  3. Preventive regulation.

Conclusion

The next digital monopoly will not be an expanded version of today's platforms. It will be the one that becomes the only viable way to process information and make decisions.
